# Focusing optics and beam propagation: fits to the logs (2026-11-13)

Code `blade/physics/optics_fit.py`, numbers `optics.json`, test `tests/test_fit_optics.py`. Citations: startup/tables docs by `doc_id:line`; user logs only as "user log <year>".

## 1. Fitted / validated

**Tilt lens (E stacks).** The code (`mpe_bluesky`, `blade.physics.lens`) uses `f = h sin(tilt)/delta`. The logs fit `f = h sin(tilt)/(c delta)` with **c = 2.11 +- 0.17** and a **focus ("sample") plane at z = 69.49 +- 0.15 m**, not 71.5 m.
- Data (4 numbers, Hf edge 65.351 keV): lens1E / lens2E optimum tilt relative to the lens zero, tomo at the sample and 205 mm downstream: 0.11875 / 0.10579 and 0.132 / 0.11865 deg [2026-mpe_jan26:L597-604] (theta = half the difference of the two +/- focus conditions; their midpoint reproduces the stored zeros -0.011 / -0.0627).
- Fit chi2 = 0.07 for 2 dof (sigma_theta = 1.5 mdeg, conservative); the fitted model reproduces the four optima to < 0.6 mdeg. The old model was off by 2.5-10 mdeg (it predicts +6 mdeg for the +205 mm move, the data show +13).
- **Why Feb 2026 gave nonsense** [2026-mpe_jan26:L606-609]: the measured shift of the optimum with detector distance is 2.2x what a thin lens with q = 3.8 m predicts. Solving for the source distance with c = 1 therefore drives p to ~1e8 m with chi2 = 38 (46 with p fixed). The cause is the formula/geometry (factor 2 in c, sample 2 m closer than 71.5 m), not delta(E): delta ~ 1/E^2 is confirmed by the E scaling below.
- **Independent support:** a 2025 user log quotes the E sample position as 69.5 m; a prior of 69.5 +- 0.3 m gives c = 2.11 +- 0.15. A 2022 startup log's slit tuple (0.110 mm DS slit gives ~0.120 mm at the sample [2022-mpe_dec22_explog:L173-178]) favours 69.5 (predicts 0.1257 mm) over 71.5 (0.1294) weakly.
- **E scaling:** theta ~ 1/E^2. Feb 2026 (Tb edge 51.935 keV): predicted 0.1879 / 0.1673 deg vs 0.184 / 0.1675 observed (user log 2026). Oct 2026 (Bi, 90.526 keV, tomo 140 mm from sample, zero assumed reset): predicted 0.057-0.067 vs 0.072 and 0.050-0.060 vs 0.054 (direction of the offset not logged). B stacks (Al, h=100 um, Ho edge 55.618 keV): predicted 0.799 / 0.782 deg for a focus on tomoB (43.0 m, assumed) vs 0.822 / 0.775 observed (user log 2026).
- **Identifiability.** Only two focal-length ratios are measured, so: p (source-lens distance) is **unconstrained** (chi2 flat from -30 m to +1000 m); z_sample and c are 99 % anti-correlated; h and c enter only as h/c = 95 +- 8 um (consistent with a 100 um effective tooth for the "200 um" Si stacks, or c = 2). With c = 2 fixed, z_sample = 69.59 m. Sensitivity of the fitted model: **15 mm of focus per mdeg** (the old note quotes 41).

**B-hutch CRL (2-D, aperture ~450 um [2024-AMsetup_nov24_explog:L48]) focus size vs energy.** `FWHM^2 = s0^2 + (b (E^2 - E_tuned^2))^2` on the Nov-2024 series (sigma px 0.78125 um/px x 2.3548) [2024-AMsetup_nov24_explog:L226,L229,L233,L237,L264]: s0 = 23.8 +- 0.5 (V) / 29.2 +- 0.5 (H) um, b = 0.096 +- 0.013 um/keV^2, E_tuned = 60.65 +- 0.19 keV, chi2 = 3.3 for 6 dof (residuals < 0.9 um). b implies an effective illuminated aperture of ~200 um, consistent with the 450 um clear aperture and a 0.43 mm beam. The spot grows 25 -> 31.5 um (V) between 61.3 and 62.3 keV purely from CRL defocus (f ~ E^2); the CRL lens count follows the same law (11.5 lenses at 65.351 keV [2024-AMsetup_nov24_explog:L275] is 1 % from 10 x (65.35/61.33)^2 if 10 were used at 61.33 keV: inferred, the log line is truncated).

**Regular vs brightness mode** (61.26 keV, B CRL -> E, tomo 0.69 um/px) [2026-Brightness_mode_feb26:L39-65]: regular 27.5 (H) / 22.9 (V) um tuned, brightness 30.1 (H) / 16.9 (V): V x0.74, H x1.09-1.15. The logged "focused beam size" is **not the source size**: spot^2 = (M s)^2 + floor^2 with M = 0.786. Assuming the same floor in both modes: regular V source FWHM in [19.7, 29.1] um, brightness V in [0, 21.5], regular H <= 35.0, brightness H in [15.6, 38.3]. Floor and source cannot be separated with one energy (e.g. brightness V source 11.5 um gives a 14 um floor). sim.json's 30 / 20 um "source size" is therefore only an upper bound for regular mode.

**Penumbra.** `sigma_pen = s (z_cam - z_ap)/z_ap` is exactly what `blade.vbl` computes (verified for all 8 apertures, tests). Resulting edge sigma at tomoE for s = 12.7 um: 21 um (WB slits), 3.3 / 2.3 um (C), 1.0 / 0.34 um (E slits): only the WB, B, and C-US slits look blurred. Fresnel scale 12 um (WB) down to 3.9 um (E DS) at 71.7 keV, i.e. 6-17 px; rim visibility exp(-2 pi^2 (sigma/period)^2) kills WB/B rims and keeps E-slit rims. `vbl`'s explicit `vis` factor is redundant with its later Gaussian smoothing but harmless.

**Hotspot.** Steering lever arm 25 m (1 urad -> 0.025 mm at the WB slits, 0.0695 mm at the E sample): tuples: -6 urad -> 150 um (user log 2023), 10 urad <-> 0.25 mm [2026-techcomm_jun26_explog:L140] (geometry gives 27 m). With the present undulator the WB-slit centre moves <= 0.02 mm over 52-81 keV within a session [2025-startup1id_jun2025_explog:L24-33], [2026-mpe_jan26:L43]: no energy slope resolvable (<~1 urad). SCU 2023 table [2023-lastlight_mpe_apr23:L238-278] shows 0.8 mm non-monotonic shifts (corrector saturation), tabulated, not modelled.
