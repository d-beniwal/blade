## 2. Assumed (not constrained by the logs)
IC gas and length (flux and gas absorption only enter as a product: logs have no absolute photon flux); 100 kHz/V converter (clip = 10 V is consistent, dark check gives 77); H/V split of sigma; ring-current linearity (one cross-check: 52 keV 206 kHz at 114 mA = 361 at 200 mA vs 353 from a 0.5^2 measurement at 200 mA, 2 %); WB slit centre sensitivity at a resonant harmonic; FS leakage shape vs X. In `flux_fit.py`, `ic_rate_from_flux` with N2, 10 cm gives 1e12 ph/s -> 188 kHz at 80.7 keV (order-of-magnitude only).

IC energy response alone is **degenerate with photon flux**: for N2 it is flat within +-10 % (50-80 keV) and rises 22 % to 90 keV; for Ar it falls ~E^-1.8 (x1.87 at 52, x0.68 at 90 keV vs 71.7). The logged trend (x2.2 at 52 keV, x0.34 at 90.5 keV) needs a photon-flux trend in either case, but an Ar-like chamber would explain about 80 % of the 52 keV excess and 40 % of the 90 keV fall. The B3/B1 ratio drops 3.3 -> 2.7 between 71.7 and 80.7 keV (cross-year), hinting that B1 and B3 have different gases.

## 3. Could not be fitted (PENDING) - physics, why it matters, data needed, effort
1. **H and V beam sigma separately; WB centre offset at a resonant harmonic.** Gaussian * erf aperture; needed because the VBL steering/centre-hunt procedures depend on which axis is narrower (V is likely narrower). Needs: a flux-vs-WB-centre scan (blade scan with the other axis fixed) on IC_B1/B3 at 0.3 mm, both axes, at one energy; 2 x 15 points, 10 min. Effort: one scan + 1 h fit.
2. **Absolute photon flux and IC gas/length.** `I = flux * e * (E/W)(mu_en/mu)(1 - e^(-mu rho L))`; lets the sim give IC counts from the real undulator flux and tell Ar-like from N2-like response. Needs: ICs' gas and length from the beamline drawing, or one calibrated diode/pin reading (A) with the same slit and energy, or the same IC read at 3 energies with the undulator flux model validated. Effort: 1 h (drawing) or 1 shift.
3. **Gain exponent to better than 0.03 and 2 uA/V - 1 nA/V range.** Needs: B3 at 5 gains in one session, fixed beam (5 readings). 10 min.
4. **Ring-current linearity** (shown only by a cross-check): need B1 vs current during a decay or fill (50-200 mA). Free during a normal fill/decay logging; scaler logging script of ~20 lines.
5. **Flux vs energy at fixed IC and fixed epoch:** two chains of different ICs, 4-5 energies each, cross-session drift 20-30 %, 42-52 and 52-61 keV have a gap or a harmonic switch (n=5, 7). Needs: B1 and B3 read together at each of 45, 52, 57, 61, 66, 72, 81, 87, 91 keV, 0.3 x 0.3 mm WB, 200 mA, 50 nA/V, 2 min each, same session; also record gap and harmonic. 30 min. Then regress on the Kim harmonic flux (undulator module) to split photon flux from gas response.
6. **Heat load vs aperture** (Th2 detuning / flux loss): only the 0.4^2 / 0.8x0.7 pair, after re-peaking. Needs: B3 vs time for 0.3^2 -> 0.8x0.7 without re-peaking, plus Th2 rocking curves at each aperture. 1 h.
7. **Kohzu-slit transmission downstream**: the angular-acceptance geometry in the sim is physics, but the logs give no flux-vs-slit-size series for B/C/E Kohzu slits (only that 0.11 mm slits give 0.12 mm penumbra). Needs: IC3-E (or 6-E) vs E-US slit size at fixed centre, 0.05-0.5 mm, and the same for C. 20 min.
8. **Attenuator thickness check per position**: one transmission point only. Needs: IC counts at a fixed energy through every wheel position (11 + 8 points) on a clean IC, dark subtracted; record E. 10 min; also resolves the 2022-era wheel (older "002 = 49" remark implies 0.94 of nominal thickness) vs 2026 tables.
9. **Fast-shutter timing** (open/close latency, jitter, dose per frame): no millisecond numbers anywhere in the logs; qualitative only (0/1/2 pulses, lag at 0.5 s frames, 10 data + 5 dark pattern). Needs: scope trace of the trigger/IC6 response, or the scaler read of a 0.01-0.1 s open command. 30 min.
10. **FS leakage vs X and close/open ratios on a clean IC:** leak shape is assumed. Needs: IC6-E vs FS X from 1.0 to 3.0 mm in 0.1 mm steps, closed, at 0.3 x 0.3 mm WB. 10 min.
11. **BPM/IC position sensitivity and the "C_hutch_beam_position_IC_scale" constant**: the string is not in the knowledge base. Only the BPM angle gain (0.88/0.92) exists. Needs the split-IC (A-B)/(A+B) vs steering scan; 15 min.
12. **Source of the cross-session spread** (20-30 %): attributable to Th2/gap tuning, ring mode (timing vs brightness) and IC drift; a ring-mode flag per row is not in the logs. Cannot be fitted; the VBL should add 5 % session noise (B3 sd 4.1 %, B1 5.8 %).
13. **B1 channel naming:** the scaler channel `ic1b` in Emon and E_scan blocks is dark-like (0.6 kHz) while "IC_B1" reads 170 kHz in the startup tables; likely a different physical chamber or the HEM low-profile unit swapped in/out. Needs the scaler channel map.

## 4. How to use it
`flux_fit.wb_flux_factor(h, v, cx, cy, x0, y0)` (flux through a WB aperture); `ic_rate_scaled(rate_ref, gain, I_mA, ic)` (rescale a pinned reading); `attenuator_transmission(wheel, pos, E)`; `flux_vs_energy_correction(E)` (multiply the 176 kHz pin); `fs_leakage_fraction(x)`; `bpm_angle_urad(xp, yp)`. Validity: 40-95 keV, resonant harmonics, WB 0.2-0.8 mm, 50-200 mA.
