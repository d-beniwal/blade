# 9. How the beamline is aligned before an experiment (from the logbooks)

Built 2026-11-11 from ~25 user and startup logs (2023-2026, read line by line in their set-up / energy-calibration / alignment / focusing sections) plus keyword search over all 121. Names of users and operators are deliberately left out (the repo is public); statements carry a tag: **[log]** read in the logs, **[inf]** this project's inference from logged numbers, **[not found]** looked for and absent. The executable version is `blade.vbl.procedures.startup_sequence` (virtual beamline, `playbooks/virtual_beamline.md`).

## 9.1 The ordered checklist (E-hutch user, standard case; 3-6 h of staff time) [log]
| # | step | where | signal watched | done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | inherit last settings; update the SPEC macros to the new edge (foil name, `E_scan` window/fit guesses) | SPEC | - | macros compile |
| 1 | WB slits to working size (0.3x0.3 ... 0.6x0.4 mm), downstream slits opened to 2x2 mm or more, attenuators and foil out | A/B/C/E | - | beam reaches the B ICs |
| 2 | undulator to the nominal gap of the harmonic (`setundgap`), HEM energy typed into the transform, HEM motors on | ID, HEM | IC_B counts | beam on the B ICs |
| 3 | pre-amp sanity check on the B ICs; zero the mu_t calculator (shutter closed -> dark terms, open without foil -> zero) | B | IC-B1/B3/B5/B8 | counts sensible, mu_t = 0 foil out |
| 4 | **gap scan at fixed energy** (`undscangap`, high -> low, 40-50 points, 1 s each, +-0.2 or +-0.1 mm); set the gap at the peak (or slightly above) | ID | IC1-B (or the IC before the foil) | peak found |
| 5 | **Th2 tweak** for maximum flux (a few 0.0002 deg steps), repeat the gap scan over the same range | HEM | IC-B1/B3/B5 | local maximum |
| 6 | **foil in, `Emon`, `E_scan`** (3-9 min): fit mu_t(E) = dif + b (1 + erf((E-pos)/wid))/2 | HEM, foil wheel | mu_t | fit converges |
| 7 | move the HEM energy (via the Th1 offset) until mu_t = fitted b, Th2 for flux, re-tweak the energy, re-check with `Emon` | HEM | mu_t | within ~0.05 of b |
| 8 | **tomo camera into the beam** (C: PG5, B: Retiga, E: PG1/GH1): the WB-slit image and the hotspot appear; compare the hotspot with the centre of the WB-slit image; if they differ request **orbit steering** (2-6 urad, horizontal quick, vertical slow) or nudge the WB slits by 10-20 um | C/B/E | hotspot px vs WB image | hotspot within ~10-15 px of the slit-image centre |
| 9 | **Kohzu slits** (B often skipped, then C us/ds, then move the E tomo in: E us/ds): close the pair to 0.1-0.2 mm, tweak H/V centre until the slit image sits on the target pixel (about 960 x 600 on a 1920x1200 frame), reopen, re-zero | B/C/E | slit image on the target pixel | centred |
| 10 | fast shutters (B, E): with FS closed toggle the PSS shutter and watch IC4/IC6 (a few % change is fine); scan the blades for their edges on the tomo (FS is 3.0 mm wide in X, 1.7 mm in Y) | B/E | IC4/IC6, tomo | no change on PSS toggle |
| 11 | IC pre-amps in E, with all slits open and again at the working beam size | E | IC1..IC8 E | above dark, not saturated |
| 12 | **rotation axis**: aeroX to the hotspot, pin onto the axis with samX/samZ (centre stays within ~1 px over 360 deg), zero angle (move samZ by 8-9 mm: ry error = asin(dx / 2dz)), wedge (< 0.02 deg), axis tilt Tx from the +90/-90 images | E | pin centre / tip row at 0, 180, +-90 | see `05_sample_stage_and_rotation_axis.md` and `blade align` |
| 13 | **lenses**: tilt (th or phi) to the narrowest/brightest line (0.0006 deg step), lens y to the optical-axis row, chi to match the axis tilt | E | focus line on tomo or NF detector | narrowest, centred |
| 14 | NF detector on the axis, near-field beamstop (touch the beam, back off one 2 um step), then far-field calibration (CeO2/LaB6) and sample height (`mid` macro) | E | NF image, rings | - |
Order caveats seen: some groups align slits before lenses, some steer after the energy, some align C while the users work in E. The sequence above is the most common one. [log]

## 9.2 How the flux is maximised, and what 'the best point in energy' is
- **The energy is not chosen by flux. It is pinned to the absorption edge of a calibration foil.** [log] The operators fit `E_scan` (parameters `b` = step, `dif` = pre-edge level, `pos`, `wid`; e.g. Tb b 0.777, Pr 1.0, Bi 1.22, Re 1.37-1.45, Yb 1.40, Au 1.37-1.39) and then move the HEM energy until the measured mu_t equals `b` (E_HEM ~ pos, 0-10 eV above it). mu_t is a 3-8 eV energy meter (slope b / (wid sqrt(pi)) ~ 0.013 per eV at Re). [inf]
- **Flux is then maximised at that energy with three knobs**, in this order: the **undulator gap** (the harmonic must sit on the mono energy; scan high -> low, set at the peak), the **second-crystal angle Th2** (tweaked by hand while watching IC-B1/B3/B5; 8 % to x1.9 gain after a beam dump), and the **orbit steering** (a few urad; re-setting the gap after steering gave ~5 %). [log] Th2 also drags the energy readout (mu_t moved 1.299 -> 1.334 at Re), so the loop is energy -> Th2 -> energy. [log] Any change of WB-slit size, gap, steering or after a dump needs a re-peak (the first crystal warms: +3 K at 0.4x0.4 mm, +10 K at 0.8x0.7 mm).
- **Gap-curve shape** (read off the embedded plots) [log]: single asymmetric peak, FWHM ~0.08 mm at n=5 (52 keV) and 0.07-0.11 mm at n=7, i.e. ~0.8 % in energy; steeper on the closing (low-gap) side, long high-gap tail; peak height depends on how well the mono is tuned (x2 between a tuned and an untuned day); a 0.04 mm miss costs 25-30 % of the flux. The peak wanders ~0.004 mm day to day and repeats only when approached from large gap (hysteresis). Counts at a tuned peak: IC-B1 ~176 kHz at 0.3x0.3 mm WB, 200 mA, 50 nA/V.
- **Peak gaps logged** (PMU era) for lookup: Re 71.676 keV n=7: 10.01-10.05 mm; Au 80.725 n=9: 8.99-9.00; Tb 51.996 n=5: 10.16-10.18; Yb 61.33 n=7: 8.825; Hf 65.35 n=7: 9.277; Bi 90.5: 9.863; Pr 41.99: 8.54; 100 keV n=11: 9.08-9.10. Taper (0.010-0.060 mm) is a second small knob set by staff. [log]
- **Edge width follows the mono's angular acceptance**: wid(E) = E x 23 urad / tan(theta_B) (Tb 0.032, Yb 0.048, Re 0.060, Au 0.073 keV). [inf from 7 fits]
- **[not found]**: a Th2 rocking curve with a plotted width (Th2 is only tweaked by hand, steps of 0.0002 deg; Th1 0.0001, Chi2 0.0005); a steering gain in um/urad (the geometry gives 0.07 mm per urad at 71.6 m; logged corrections of 2-6 urad moved the hotspot by 0.1-0.3 mm between WB slit and tomo, as expected); the reason for setting the gap 'slightly above' the peak.

## 9.3 What the tomo camera shows in these steps (log screenshots)
- Hotspot + WB-slit image: the footprint is a rounded rectangle (up to ~1.1 x 1.0 mm for WB 0.5-0.6 mm), brightest at the hotspot and falling 3-10x toward the edges; vertical-streak mottle (50-150 px, +-15-30 %), +-5-8 % pixel speckle, fixed-pattern dust/scratches that stay on the sensor when the beam moves, dark floor 0.5-1.5 kcounts, a 1-5 % scatter halo.
- **Sharp vs blurry edges**: an edge defined by a **Kohzu slit in hutch E** is crisp (erf sigma ~4 px at 0.78 um/px, 10-90 % ~10 px, with a bright rim of a few px); an edge defined by the **WB slits** or a B slit is a 60-100 px ramp. The geometric penumbra is the source size times (camera distance - slit distance)/slit distance, so the slit closest to the camera wins. No diffraction fringes between tight slits were found at 69-100 um; closing a slit to tens of um gives a smooth dim top-hat with 10-20 px edges. [log]
- Pin: dark truncated cone, apex up, full angle ~30 deg (half 14-15), flat top 130-160 px (110-150 um), interior 8-20 % transmission (darker toward the base), bright 1-3 px rim along the flanks, 2-4 px edges in focus. [log]
- Camera partly in the beam: one steep edge and a smooth gradient toward the other side; the sensor boundary cuts the beam image. No log image sequence of the hot-spot search exists. [log]
- Camera stages (soft limits, in = 0 after zeroing on the hotspot): tomoC x 1idc:m79 (-225..31, out about -225 to -250), y m84; tomoE us x 1ide1:m63 (-1..284, out ~150), y m40, z m62; tomoE ds x m111 (-0.7..208), y m112. Pixel sizes: 1.17 (PG1/PG5, 5X), 0.69-0.70 (GH1), 0.78 (7.5X), 1.48 (NF Retiga). [log]
